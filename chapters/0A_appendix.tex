
Enterprise dataset is generated by crawling the customer facing open web-pages of the partner enterprise. Crawler is based on the Breat-First search using the Jsoup -library. The crawler is configured to use only Finish language content for household segment, and thus other languages and other customer segments are left out to limit the scope of the dataset. All the styling and not main content related elements are removed in the best effort, to have as clean pages as possible. As the simulated customer questions have questions related to terms and conditions of the products of the partner company, also PDF's which includes the T\&C of the products are also included in the crawling. The final raw dataset contains 250 web-pages and 50 PDF documents converted into markdown files. The code and the dataset can be found from <link to Git>.

As the MuSiQue dataset has strict predefined structure, the enterprise dataset is converted to follow same structure to keep the comparability of two different datasets as close as possible. Final enterprise dataset is constructed in the following way:

First, the full raw dataset is ingested in the vector database using methodology defined in ~\ref{sec:methods}. Then, for each question, Google Gemini Flash 3.8 generative AI model is used to select 0, 1 or 2 supportive chunks and 20, 19 or 18 distraction chunks. In addition, same model is used to generate 20 out-of-domain questions that are related to competitors or other industry related questions, that cannot be answered based on the facts in the database. Lastly, the new dataset is converted to follow excatly same structure as the Musique --dataset.

The conversion is done, because the sizes of the corpuses have very different scopes, as the Musique contains 100 question * 20 text chunks = 2000 text chunks and when duplicates are cleaned, the final corpus is ~1700 text chunks <What is the excact number?>. After the conversion, the cleaned enterprise dataset contains 1333 unique text chunks, while raw dataset contains <What is the number?> unqiue text chunks. Test statistics for the raw dataset is found in the appendix~\ref{sec:appendix_c}.
