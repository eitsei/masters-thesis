\mysection{AI usage}

Large language models (LLMs) and generative artificial intelligence tools, specifically Google Gemini, were utilized in multiple phases of this thesis. First, they assisted in the ideation phase to refine
the research scope. Second, they were used to identify relevant literature, keywords, and search formulations for the literature review. Third, AI tools were employed for grammatical correction and to improve the stylistic flow and vocabulary of the thesis; however, all draft text was originally authored by the author. Lastly, AI-based code assistants were used to support code development for the empirical evaluation.

The author accepts full responsibility for the factual accuracy of this thesis and for any errors or omissions, whether originating from the author's own input or from the systems utilized.
